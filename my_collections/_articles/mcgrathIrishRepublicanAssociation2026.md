abstract: "This article offers a reappraisal of the largest Irish organisation in\
  \ South African history: the Irish Republican Association of South Africa (IRASA).\
  \ It draws upon previously unexplored associational material and personal correspondence\
  \ to shine new light upon the group\u2019s origin, its ideological makeup and, ultimately,\
  \ its shocking collapse in the summer of 1922. It argues that although the IRASA\
  \ enjoyed significant early success \u2014 especially within a South African context\
  \ \u2014 from the outset it had been set upon an unstable foundation and, with time,\
  \ subsidence set in. By reconsidering such an organisation, this article develops\
  \ our understanding of the Irish population in South Africa and, following recent\
  \ calls by historians, it provides new insights into the global imprints that were\
  \ left behind by the Irish Revolution."
