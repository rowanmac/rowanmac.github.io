abstract: Catalonia and Ireland are often mentioned in debates regarding sovereignty
  and how independence is claimed. Nevertheless, the reception of the 1916 Easter
  Rising by the contemporaneous Catalan-language press has not been documented and
  both the subsequent interpretation of the events and the impact on Catalan politics
  remain understudied. To illustrate the varied commentary and criticism across different
  sections of society, this article explores the presentation and interpretation of
  the Easter Rising in Catalan-language news outlets. The article focuses first on
  major newspapers in Barcelona, before turning to smaller publications beyond booming
  cities or within specific ideological and cultural camps, and then addresses the
  Catalanophone world beyond Europe. This article underscores how the Rising was quickly
  appropriated by different entities across the political spectrum in order to advance
  their own agendas within the Catalan-speaking world.
