abstract: "By examining the histories of anti-abortion activism in Belgium, Ireland,\
  \ Poland, and Spain, the special issue \u2018Anti-abortion activism in Catholic\
  \ Europe (1970s\u2013present)\u2019 reorients away from the principal focus of most\
  \ research on \u2018pro-life\u2019 activism: in historical scholarship, the United\
  \ States undoubtedly stands as the geographic and symbolic center of the global\
  \ \u2018pro-life\u2019 movement. The contributions to this special issue explore\
  \ contemporary histories of anti-abortion activisms in four European countries with\
  \ diverse political regimes, in which the majority of citizens are Catholic and\
  \ the Catholic Church exerts extensive political and social influence. After summarizing\
  \ existing literature on the history of \u2018pro-life\u2019 movements, we discuss\
  \ the main analytical lenses that have informed this collection: gender and generation.\
  \ We also reflect on gaps in the literature, source availability, and methodological\
  \ issues that arise when constructing the \u2018anti-abortion\u2019 archive, before\
  \ outlining the contributions to this special issue."
