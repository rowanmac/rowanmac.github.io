abstract: "This article considers two previously overlooked texts, both printed in\
  \ Dublin during the reign of Charles II by an otherwise unknown Quaker author named\
  \ Michael Harward, who had emigrated from rural England to Ireland in the 1650s.\
  \ The first is an almanac for 1666, the only known copy of which was recently purchased\
  \ by the National Library of Ireland. The almanac contains much of interest for\
  \ its readers, from tide-times at Dublin bar and the times and places of markets\
  \ throughout Ireland to detailed suggestions for the cultivation of crops and domestic\
  \ economy. Harward\u2019s other book, *The Herds-man\u2019s Mate* (1673) is a comprehensive\
  \ manual for treating ailments in cattle. It contains scores of herbal and surgical\
  \ remedies of all kinds interwoven with entertaining anecdotes drawn from Harward\u2019\
  s veterinary experiences and his interactions with neighbours in Co Offaly.</p>"
