abstract: "The belief in a \u2018natural\u2019 Irish sympathy with the Palestinian\
  \ people, based on a shared history of colonisation, is a very widespread one. Indeed,\
  \ many suggest that the legacy of resistance to British rule in Ireland produces\
  \ an automatic solidarity with other oppressed peoples. Current Ulster Unionist\
  \ support for Israel seems to confirm this trope, but Unionist attitudes towards\
  \ this issue have also been fluid. Irish republicans have not always identified\
  \ with the Palestinian cause and indeed at times some even embraced militant Zionism.\
  \ Irish support for the Palestinians is real but has diverse roots and is much more\
  \ recent than many assume."
