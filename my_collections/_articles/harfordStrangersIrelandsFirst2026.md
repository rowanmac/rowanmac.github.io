abstract: "This article explores the lives of Ireland\u2019s first women professors,\
  \ whose careers spanned the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, a period\
  \ marked by the international expansion of higher education for women, against the\
  \ backdrop of wider social, economic and political change. Drawing on a range of\
  \ sources, including university records, personal correspondence, and student testimonies,\
  \ the article interrogates how these women navigated a university landscape built\
  \ on male privilege and norms. Marginalised through an architecture of control,\
  \ both spatial and ideological, the article reveals the strategies these women employed\
  \ in order to gain an intellectual foothold and build an institutional presence."
