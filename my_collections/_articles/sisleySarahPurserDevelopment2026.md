abstract: "The painter Sarah Purser (1848\u20131943) was an indomitable figure in\
  \ the Irish cultural scene of her day. She played a significant role in two of Dublin\u2019\
  s art museums, the Municipal Gallery of Modern Art (now Hugh Lane Gallery) and the\
  \ National Gallery of Ireland (NGI). A close friend of the art dealer, collector,\
  \ and philanthropist Hugh Lane, Purser was pivotal in the founding of the Municipal\
  \ Gallery and she later secured Charlemont House for its permanent home. She served\
  \ on the Board of Governors and Guardians of the NGI from 1909 to 1943, providing\
  \ continuity during turbulent times. Her sharp intellect, knowledge of art, and\
  \ social connections ensured that she was a valued advocate for both the NGI and\
  \ Hugh Lane Gallery across four decades. A significant collector herself, she established\
  \ the Friends of the National Collections of Ireland to support public museums,\
  \ with the Municipal Gallery and the NGI being among the first beneficiaries of\
  \ this philanthropy. Under her leadership the Friends campaigned for the return\
  \ to Dublin of the paintings in the Sir Hugh Lane Bequest 1917 (now shared in partnership\
  \ between the National Gallery, London, and Hugh Lane Gallery). She also founded\
  \ and managed the stained-glass studio An T\xFAr Gloine and helped to organise numerous\
  \ exhibitions."
